The 2025 Stark Varg SM is Stark Future’s street-legal electric supermoto, the third model in the Varg family after the MX (motocross) and EX (enduro). It keeps the same 360 V carbon-sleeved motor and 7.2 kWh structural battery, then swaps the dirt-bike wheels and travel for 17-inch street rubber, a shorter KYB fork, a Brembo Stylema front brake, and road lighting. Stark claims the best power-to-weight ratio of any production supermoto.

It was formally unveiled in October 2025 as a plated hooligan bike for street and kart-track use, not a dual-sport.

Powertrain and Performance

Motor: Liquid-cooled PMAC in a carbon-fiber housing, about 360 V
Power: 60 hp Standard or 80 hp Alpha (+$1,000 software unlock)
Torque: Claimed about 674 lb-ft (914 Nm) at the rear wheel
Drive: Single-speed with RK gold 520 chain
Control: Power, regen, and maps are set on the handlebar-mounted Android phone
Output Scaling: Can be scaled from beginner levels up to full Alpha

Battery, Range, and Charging

Battery: 7.2 kWh honeycomb magnesium pack (stressed member in the chassis)
Claimed range: About 50.3 miles on the WMTC cycle; Stark also quotes higher mixed-urban figures (up to the mid-70s) at conservative power
Highway range: is shorter
Charging: About 1–2 hours on 240 V with the 3.3 kW portable charger or stand; much longer on 120 V

Range is cited at 53 miles/87 km highway or 73 miles/118 km combined city/highway miles.

The 2025 Varg SM is the paved version of Stark’s electric dirt-bike formula: 80 hp, absurd rear-wheel torque, KYB/Brembo hardware, and a weight that embarrasses most petrol supermotos.
